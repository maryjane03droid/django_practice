Pregnancy Tips

  • Attend antenatal clinic visits regularly.
  • Eat balanced meals with fruits, vegetables, proteins, and enough water.
  • Rest when tired and avoid carrying very heavy loads.
  • Talk to a health worker if you feel severe pain, bleeding, dizziness, or reduced baby movement.
  • Prepare your hospital bag early.
  • Keep emergency contacts nearby.
